# Ducks: search, rights and clarity review, 27 October 2026

Reviewed by `claude` on 27 October 2026 for the ducks concept pool in [stimulus-concepts.md](../../../docs/stimulus-concepts.md) (three ordinary members, three impossible members, three borderline cases and three non-members).

## Search

Unsplash and Pexels were searched on 27 October 2026; 505 unique results were screened (478 Unsplash, 27 Pexels). Mallards, domestic ducks, mandarin ducks, swans, penguins and pigeons were easy to find. A rubber duck on a pond was not: every pond or lake shot showed the duck too small, several ducks, a crowd or a non-classic duck, so the one usable photograph is a yellow rubber duck in a swimming pool. No wooden decoy floating on water exists on either site (decoys appear on shelves, in fields or in hunting scenes), and no photograph shows the impossible ducks or a mechanical duck, so these were generated. The first renders of the rubber, robot and decoy ducks placed them among real ducks, which would make each image a plain target, so they were rejected and the robot duck and decoy were re-rendered alone; the first duck reading a newspaper showed a person's hands beside the duck and was re-rendered with the hands growing from the duck.
