# Rights and clarity review, 27 October 2026

Reviewed by `claude` on 27 October 2026. Sixteen replacement photographs were found through Unsplash searches with the Unsplash connector (Pexels and Pixabay pages refused automated access from this environment). About 490 results were screened at thumbnail size against the [target clarity standard](../../../docs/stimuli.md#target-clarity-standard), 70 were inspected at 1280 px, and a square crop was planned for each shortlisted photograph. Common reasons for rejection were a landscape frame too wide for a square crop with margins, obstructions (posts, racks, railings), low contrast, people, steep views and cluttered settings.

For each photograph below, the individual publisher page was fetched and names the creator and states "Free to use under the Unsplash License"; the page's image key matches the reviewed preview. The Unsplash API record marks each photograph as free, not Unsplash+.
