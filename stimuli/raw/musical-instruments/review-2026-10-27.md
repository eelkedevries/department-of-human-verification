# Musical instruments: search, rights and clarity review, 27 October 2026

Reviewed by `claude` on 27 October 2026 for the musical instruments concept pool in [stimulus-concepts.md](../../../docs/stimulus-concepts.md) (three ordinary members, three impossible members, three borderline cases and three non-members).

## Search

Unsplash and Pexels were searched on 27 October 2026; about 930 results were screened. Violins, trumpets and an accordion were found; most trumpet photographs were too long for a square crop. No photograph of saucepans set up as a drum kit was found in about 270 results, and no bicycle floor pump on its own was found in about 250 results (Pexels returns only people pumping tyres). The spade and paint roller are non-members; after further searches for a pump and a megaphone (40 results), a galvanised watering can (Unsplash, 24 results screened) substitutes for the pump as the third. The impossible instruments, the hose horn, the vacuum-cleaner bagpipes and the saucepan drums were generated. The first renders of the self-playing piano and the saxophone with a mouth (each showed a person playing), the saucepan drums (an ordinary drum kit) and the vacuum bagpipes (an unrecognisable canister) were rejected; all four were re-rendered successfully.
