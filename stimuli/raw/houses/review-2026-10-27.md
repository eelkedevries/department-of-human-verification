# Houses: search, rights and clarity review, 27 October 2026

Reviewed by `claude` on 27 October 2026 for the houses concept pool in [stimulus-concepts.md](../../../docs/stimulus-concepts.md) (three ordinary members, three impossible members, three borderline cases and three non-members).

## Search

Unsplash and Pexels were searched on 27 October 2026; 996 unique results were screened. Terraced and detached houses were straightforward; neighbouring houses show at the crop edges for the terraced house, as is unavoidable. Bungalows are wide and low, leaving much sky or foreground in a square crop (marginal). Most office-tower photographs were steep upward shots or cut at the base; most bus shelters had people, adverts or houses behind them; single unmodified shipping containers are rare. No photograph shows the impossible houses or the borderline dwellings, so these were generated. The first renders of the house walking in boots (the boots walked apart from the house), the hermit crab (it stood beside the house) and the curling front wall (no curl) were rejected; the first two were re-rendered successfully, while the curling wall, retried as an edit of the blue-grey detached house, produced a rolled sheet in front of the house and was rejected, so that item has no image.
