# Lamps: search, rights and clarity review, 27 October 2026

Reviewed by `claude` on 27 October 2026 for the lamps concept pool in [stimulus-concepts.md](../../../docs/stimulus-concepts.md) (three ordinary members, three impossible members, three borderline cases and three non-members).

## Search

Unsplash and Pexels were searched on 27 October 2026; 751 unique results were screened (622 Unsplash, 129 Pexels). Desk, floor and bedside lamps were found, though many lamp photographs are dark, low-key interiors. Vases, coffee grinders and watering cans were found; several are studio product shots. No usable photograph of a jar of fireflies used as a lamp was found, and no photograph shows the impossible lamps, the anglerfish lamp or a traffic light used as a bedside lamp, so these were generated. The first renders of the walking floor lamp (a person carrying a lamp), the staircase of light (a plain desk lamp) and the anglerfish (a fish that did not read as a light) were rejected. The walking lamp was then made by editing the floor-lamp photograph and the staircase was re-rendered; the anglerfish's second render lit the tank with a desk lamp rather than the fish's lure and was rejected, so that item has no image.
