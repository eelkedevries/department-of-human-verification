## Search

Stone arch footbridges, a suspension bridge and a wooden footbridge were found; almost every other suspension-bridge result was a deck-level or distant view. The impossible bridges were generated; the first renders of the fallen tree with a handrail and of the chain of boats did not read as crossings, so set C14@1 uses the two photographed borderline crossings. The fallen tree's second render was registered and replaces the thin photographed log in set C14@2; the chain of boats' second render showed a jetty ending mid-river and was rejected, so that item has no image.
