# Computers: search, rights and clarity review, 27 October 2026

Reviewed by `claude` on 27 October 2026 for the computers concept pool in [stimulus-concepts.md](../../../docs/stimulus-concepts.md) (three ordinary members, three impossible members, three borderline cases and three non-members).

## Search

Unsplash and Pexels were searched on 27 October 2026; about 780 results were screened. Laptops and tablets were found; full desktop set-ups rarely fit a square crop, so the chosen desktop fills only the upper half of the tile (marginal). Radios, toasters and microwave ovens were found. No photograph shows the impossible computers or the borderline hybrids (potato computer, calculator with monitor, typewriter with touchscreen), so these were generated. The first renders of the laptop with arms from its screen (a person typing), the potato computer (the potato too small to see) and the touchscreen typewriter (a plain typewriter) were rejected; the potato and typewriter were re-rendered successfully, while the laptop's second render again showed a person's arms typing and was rejected, so that item has no image.
