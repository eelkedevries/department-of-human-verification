# Computers: search, rights and clarity review, 27 October 2026

Reviewed by `claude` on 27 October 2026 for the computers concept pool in [stimulus-concepts.md](../../../docs/stimulus-concepts.md) (three ordinary members, three impossible members, three borderline cases and three non-members).

## Search

Unsplash and Pexels were first searched on 27 October 2026 (about 780 results). The theme was then redone the same day after the owner's correction that a screen or monitor on its own is not a computer: impossible computers must show the computer itself (a laptop, a tablet or a tower), a standalone monitor is a non-member, and an all-in-one computer, whose computer is hidden in the display, is debatable. The generated monitor holding an ocean was reassessed as a non-member, the all-in-one photograph and the generated all-in-one with a miniature office as debatable, and the typewriter carrying a tablet as a target (the tablet is a computer); set C10@1, which had counted the monitor and the all-in-one as computers, is superseded by C10@2. A new Unsplash search ("laptop", "desktop computer tower", "pc tower case", "raspberry pi board"; 120 results screened) added a white laptop, a desktop tower with its side panel removed and a bare single-board computer, which replaces the typewriter as the third borderline concept. The impossible computers were made by editing the space-grey laptop photograph (two arms typing out of its screen) and the desktop photograph (a miniature office inside the glass-sided tower), and by generating a laptop pouring out a real ocean; of six renders, three were rejected (arms from the side of the frame, a weaker ocean render and a tower edit that also changed the monitor). Earlier, the laptop with arms had failed twice as a generated image, the potato and typewriter needed second renders, and radios, toasters and microwave ovens were found as non-members.
