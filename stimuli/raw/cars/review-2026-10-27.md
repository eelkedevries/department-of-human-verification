## Search

Unsplash and Pexels were searched on 27 October 2026; 722 unique results were screened. Side views of hatchbacks, estate cars and convertibles were found; most side views of long cars do not fit a square crop. The motorcycle, bicycle and wheelbarrow first chosen had already been registered for other themes, so a Pexels motorcycle (17963040) was added and the registered bicycle and wheelbarrow are reused. No photograph shows the impossible cars, the foot-powered buggy, the trolley cabin or the enclosed mobility scooter, so these were generated. Two first renders were rejected: the car on horse legs came out on ordinary wheels (redone as an edit of the estate-car photograph) and the enclosed mobility scooter read as a plain small car (re-rendered); neither is part of set C04@1.
