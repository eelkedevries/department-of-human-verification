# Clocks: search, rights and clarity review, 27 October 2026

Reviewed by `claude` on 27 October 2026 for the clocks concept pool in [stimulus-concepts.md](../../../docs/stimulus-concepts.md) (three ordinary members, three impossible members, three borderline cases and three non-members).

## Search

Unsplash and Pexels were searched on 27 October 2026; 492 unique results were screened (428 Unsplash, 64 Pexels). Wall, alarm and longcase clocks were found; the longcase and twin-bell clocks are frontal views, as these objects are normally seen. Most sundial photographs are too tight to crop square, and no sundial on a window sill was found, so the chosen sundial stands on a pedestal in a park (marginal). No photograph shows the impossible clocks, a smartphone hung as a wall clock or an hourglass in a clock case, so these were generated. Three first renders were rejected: the clock with human arms as hands showed a person holding the clock, the cuckoo bird perched on the case instead of bursting through its door (both re-rendered successfully and registered after set C08@1 had been recorded), and the window-sill sundial was an unreadable stone block (the photographed sundial is used instead); none is part of set C08@1.
