## Search

Unsplash and Pexels were searched on 27 October 2026; 711 unique results were screened (612 Unsplash, 99 Pexels). Single trainers, dress shoes and wellington boots were found, several as studio shots. A horseshoe on a living horse's hoof was found as a close-up in which the leg necessarily continues past the crop. No photograph of a sock with a moulded outdoor sole met the clarity standard, and no photograph shows the impossible shoes or a banana shoe, so these were generated. Several non-members carry small brand lettering, recorded as limitations. The first renders of the walking boots (worn by a person), the trainer on tiny trainers (a ridged sole), the banana shoe (a plain yellow shoe) and the horseshoe (a metal band around the hoof) were rejected; the first three were re-rendered successfully, and the photographed horseshoe on a hoof is used for the fourth.
