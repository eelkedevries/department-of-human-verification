# Bicycle suitable level

Thirty-one ranked entries referencing unchanged raw versions, each meeting the target clarity standard, with approved visual review and individual publisher/licence checks for game use and later modifications. Their proposed crops and preparation limitations are part of the review. Sixteen were found on 27 October 2026 (ten in the clarity search, six in the set-level variety review); seventeen earlier entries were rejected for clarity. Five side-profile bicycles briefly moved out for set variety were restored as ranks 21–25, as there is no reserve status. Six three-quarter views in open settings were added later on 27 October 2026 as ranks 26–31: four from Unsplash, one from Pexels and one Flickr photograph under CC BY-NC 2.0, found through Openverse. See the source reviews of [26 October](../../raw/bicycles/rights-review-2026-10-26.md) and [27 October](../../raw/bicycles/rights-review-2026-10-27.md).

Observe each source's recorded conditions, such as credit, non-commercial use, standalone redistribution and no use in training datasets. All thirty-one have standardised versions. One, the lavender bicycle against a white brick wall, has an AI-edited derivative with hexagonal wheels in `manipulated/bicycles/`. Suitable selection does not publish the images.
